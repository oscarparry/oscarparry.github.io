Who I am and what this site is.

I am Oscar Parry. I work for the Socialist Party of England and Wales, where my job covers political education, electoral coordination for the Trade Unionist and Socialist Coalition, press and media work, and writing for the party's publications.

Most of what is on this site first appeared in the Socialist, the party's weekly paper, or in Socialism Today, its monthly magazine. Some of it has not appeared anywhere: leadoffs written for schools and meetings, which are speaking texts rather than articles, and which seemed worth keeping somewhere they can be read.

Before this I read physics at the University of Manchester and took a master's in social anthropology at the London School of Economics. An essay from that period, on carbon inequality in the global economy, won the LSE Phelan US Centre's climate change essay competition and is on this site with the video that went with it. More of the anthropology writing will follow.

The other thing here is a materialist map of ideas, a side project: a timeline of works ranked by how much of what came after depends on them, with the connections drawn in.

You can find me on GitHub. Views expressed here are my own.
