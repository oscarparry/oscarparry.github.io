A materialist map of ideas

913 works on one timeline, ranked by how much of what came after depends on them, with 1,273 connections drawn between them and 103 movements banded underneath.

Six columns run down the page, one for each domain: philosophy, science and mathematics, political economy, politics and the movement, literature, and art, music and architecture. A work sits at its date and at a height set by its weight, which is an estimate of how much of what followed depends on it. Lines are typed connections, and the dashed ones are the arises-from relations, where a work is there because the conditions that produced it are there. Click a movement bar and its works light up across every column. Selections go into the address bar, so a particular argument on the map is something you can send someone.

It is built from a spreadsheet by a script, and the whole thing rebuilds with one command, so it changes as the reading does.
