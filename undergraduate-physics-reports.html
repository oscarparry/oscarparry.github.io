Undergraduate physics reports

1 April 2020 Physics

Nine lab and coursework reports from my physics degree at Manchester, kept as PDFs so the equations and graphs survive intact.

These are laboratory and coursework reports written between 2018 and 2019 during my physics degree at the University of Manchester. They are posted as PDFs rather than web pages because the equations, error bars and plots do not survive being flattened into text, and a lab report without its graphs is not worth reading.

Several were performed and written in collaboration with lab partners, who are credited on the reports themselves.

Laboratory reports

  • Experiments with a spectrometer (February 2018)
  • Forced oscillations (April 2018) - measuring the amplitude, phase and decay of a driven phosphor-bronze bar, and the quality factor of the system at four levels of damping.
  • Heights of lunar mountains (November 2018)
  • Experiments with an optical Michelson interferometer (December 2018)
  • Interaction of beta particles and X-rays in matter (March 2019)
  • Spin echo NMR (October 2019)
  • Measuring the dissociation energy of iodine by absorption spectroscopy (November 2019)
